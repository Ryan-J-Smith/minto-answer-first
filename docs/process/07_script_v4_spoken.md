# Script v4 — spoken draft (for the director's text audit; NOT yet recorded)

Why v4: the director found v3 unnatural (it did not read as natural speech), with
fragments that read like slide text, and three factual/tonal faults:
- "a woman at McKinsey worked out why [the room stopped listening]" — false; she did not research
  audience attention. She was asked to fix writing and concluded the real problem was thinking.
- "the most durable idea in management consulting" — overreach.
- "Explain it in a minute" — nonsense; "learn it in minutes, master it over a career" was meant.
- HBS: say plainly that she attended and graduated; the entrance exam is immaterial.

Voice for v4: a documentary narrator, or someone presenting to a room. Full sentences. Natural
connectives ("but", "and", "so", "instead"). Lists are spoken, not read off a slide. Pauses fall
where a speaker would breathe, not after every clause. Facts trace to `01_research_dossier.md`;
firm-internal claims carry `[CHECK]`.

Beat = one synthesised unit followed by `pause` seconds.

| # | Narration | pause |
|---|-----------|------:|
| 1 | The technique itself is one shape and three rules. | 0.8 |
| 2 | You start with the answer. Not the background, not the story of how you got there. The answer. Beneath it go the reasons, and beneath those, the evidence. | 0.8 |
