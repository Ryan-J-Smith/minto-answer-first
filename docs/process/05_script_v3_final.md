# Script v3 (locked for build) — "Answer First"

Merged from `reviews/editor_review_v2.md` and `reviews/viewer_review_v2.md`. Narration text is
authoritative in `src/scenes.json` (beats + pauses). This file adds the picture per beat.

Decisions taken
- **No photographs.** Director could not find a reusable photo of Minto. Origin is typographic/animated.
- **Hook keeps its promise:** the completed one-page pyramid is on screen before the title.
- **Chapters open with their answer** (Origin: "It started with a woman with no degree…";
  Technique: "One shape. Three rules."; Reach: "It went everywhere consultants went.").
- **Reach is Minto's reach.** CEO tally dropped. One diffusion number (~100,000) kept; flagged.
- **SCQA jargon dropped.** Reveal says "A problem. A question. Then the answer."
- **Author appears once,** on screen only, in scene 11: *[author line, later cut]* `[CHECK: director to approve/rewrite]`
- Voice: Kokoro `bf_emma`, speed 1.0. Every beat is followed by its scripted pause.
- Running example header on screen from 05 onward: **"To: the CEO · Re: pricing"** (never narrated).
- Five-slide spine: 1 thesis · 2 origin · 3 technique (05–08) · 4 reach · 5 next steps.

Visual language (all scenes)
- Paper, ink, one accent. Hairline boxes. Serif for answers and big numbers; sans for everything else.
- Nothing on screen repeats the narration except the captions.
- Motion: ease-out entrances (no bounce), camera pushes with ease-in-out, draw-on connectors.
- Persistent chapter marker: small eyebrow top-left "SLIDE n OF 5 · NAME".
- Captions bottom-centre; keep content above y=880.
