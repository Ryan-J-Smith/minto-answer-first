# Script v2 + storyboard — "Answer First"

Status: DRAFT for team review. v1 (`02_script.md`) was rejected: hook weak, picture was text-on-screen.

Format notes
- Narration is written in **beats**. Each beat is synthesised separately and followed by a real
  silence (`pause`, seconds). Pauses are part of the writing, not an afterthought.
- Voice: Kokoro `bf_emma`, speed ~0.95.
- Picture column is what the viewer sees **while** that beat is spoken. Nothing on screen may be the
  narration text itself. Captions are the only text that echoes the voice.
- Running example (scenes 05–08): a pricing recommendation.
  - Answer: **Raise the price of the core plan by 8% in January.**
  - Supports (MECE, 3Cs): **Customers will stay** · **Competitors already moved** · **We need the margin**
  - Evidence under Customers: 94% renewed after the last increase · survey: value rated 4.6/5 · churn risk concentrated in one segment we can grandfather
  - Evidence under Competitors: two of three rivals raised 6–10% this year · our price is now lowest in the category · no share loss when they moved
  - Evidence under Company: cost base up 11% · margin target missed two quarters · 8% closes the gap
- Question form (issue tree, scene 08): Should we raise the price? → Will customers stay? / What have competitors done? / Do we need it?

Target: ≤ 520 narrated words. Ceiling 4:00 total including pauses, title, credits.
